# Chief Technology Officer (CTO) Job Description

## Company
**Next Experience Digital Solution (NXDSOL)**

## Position
**Chief Technology Officer (CTO)**

## Location
Remote

## About Us
Next Experience Digital Solution (NXDSOL) is a cutting-edge company focused on developing AI-powered digital solutions. Our mission is to integrate generative AI into workflows and products, deploy conversational AI strategies, and create AI-powered automation to reduce operational drag. We are looking for a visionary CTO to lead our technical strategy and build a world-class engineering team.

## Job Description
As the CTO of NXDSOL, you will be responsible for:
- Leading the technical strategy and vision for the company.
- Building and managing a high-performing engineering team.
- Overseeing the development of AI-powered digital solutions, including AI Content Templates and Digital Business Planner.
- Ensuring the scalability, security, and performance of our products.
- Collaborating with the CEO and other stakeholders to define and execute the company's roadmap.

## Qualifications
- Proven experience as a CTO or in a similar leadership role in the tech industry.
- Strong background in full-stack development and AI/ML technologies.
- Experience in building and managing high-performing engineering teams.
- Excellent leadership, communication, and project management skills.
- Passion for innovation and a vision for the future of AI-powered digital solutions.

## Responsibilities
- Develop and implement the technical strategy for NXDSOL.
- Lead the design, development, and deployment of AI-powered digital solutions.
- Hire, mentor, and manage the engineering team.
- Ensure the scalability, security, and performance of our products.
- Collaborate with the CEO and other stakeholders to define and execute the company's roadmap.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and technologies in AI and digital solutions.

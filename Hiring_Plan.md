# NXDSOL Hiring Plan

## Phase 1: Founding Engineer (CTO) - Immediate

**Role:** Chief Technology Officer  
**Timeline:** Week 1-2  
**Priority:** Critical

### Job Requirements
- Full-stack development expertise
- AI/ML experience (generative AI, conversational AI)
- Leadership and team building skills
- Product development experience

### Hiring Process
1. Define role and requirements (Complete)
2. Create job description (Complete)
3. Interview and evaluate candidates
4. Extend offer and onboard

## Phase 2: Engineering Team - Q1

Once CTO is onboard, they will lead hiring for:

### Senior Full-Stack Engineer
- React/Node.js expertise
- API design and development
- 3-5 years experience

### AI/ML Engineer
- Generative AI integration
- Prompt engineering
- ML model deployment

---

## Phase 3: Product & Design - Q2

### Product Designer
- UI/UX design
- Design systems
- User research

### Product Manager
- Roadmap planning
- Stakeholder management
- Agile methodology

---

## Budget Allocation
- CTO: 40% of engineering budget
- Senior Engineers: 35%
- AI/ML Engineer: 15%
- Product & Design: 10%

## Success Metrics
- Time to hire (target: <30 days for CTO)
- Quality of hire (performance reviews at 90 days)
- Team velocity (features shipped per sprint)
- Employee retention rate

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

You can ask ChatGPT for a "good LinkedIn post about X" and spend 20 minutes formatting the result. Our templates are pre-validated structures — hooks, character limits, subject-line length, CTA placement — with your variables wired in. You're not prompting; you're filling in a form that already knows what a good post looks like.

Can I really regenerate just one section?

Yes. Each AI-generated block is independently regenerable. Fix the conclusion without touching the intro. The rest of your document stays exactly as it was.

What formats can I export to?

Markdown and HTML on every tier. PDF and DOCX on Pro and Team. Everything copies to clipboard cleanly.

What does "generation" mean for usage caps?

One generation = one click of the Generate button, regardless of how many blocks the template has. Block-level regeneration is included and doesn't double-count.

What happens if I hit my monthly limit on Free?

You get a notice. Nothing is blocked or deleted — you can still edit existing outputs. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited, or wait for the monthly reset.

Can my team collaborate?

Pro supports up to 5 seats. Team supports 10, with SSO. Everyone in a workspace shares the same template library and generated outputs.

What happens to my data?

Your variables and generated outputs live in your workspace. We don't train on your content.

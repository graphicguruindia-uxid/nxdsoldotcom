## Outreach Sequence (Per Contact)

| 1 | Day 1 | Email | Segment-specific |

| 4 | Day 14 | LinkedIn message | "Saw your recent post on {topic} — relevant to what we're building" |
| 5 | Day 21 | Email final | "Last note — beta spots filling. Let me know if interested." |

**Tracking:** Open rate, reply rate, meeting booked rate

---

## Response Handling

| Response Type | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| **Yes/Interested** | Book 15-min demo (Calendly) → send Loom + beta access form |
| **Maybe/Later** | Add to nurture sequence (monthly updates) |
| **No/Not interested** | Thank, ask for referral, remove from list |
| **No response (3 touches)** | Move to "cold" list, re-engage in 90 days |
| **Referral** | Thank referrer, prioritize new contact |

---

## Demo Script (15 Minutes)

1. **Context (2 min):** "You're producing X content pieces/month. Here's where teams get stuck."
2. **Problem (3 min):** Blank page → generic AI → no brand voice → manual edits
3. **Solution (5 min):** Live demo — pick template → fill context → generate → block regen → brand voice
4. **Beta Details (3 min):** Timeline, pricing, what we need from you (weekly 15-min feedback)
5. **Next Steps (2 min):** Sign up form, Slack/Discord invite, first template assignment
